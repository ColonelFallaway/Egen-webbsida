Roblox RPG spel

Rougeblox

This is possibly the best rpg game on roblbox, filled with exploration fantastic enemies, great pvp and a unique story, this all adds together in a very difficult game that before had permadeath

Pilgrammed

Pilgrammed is a very open world rpg filled with fun builds with limitless possibilities

Voxlblade

A Blocky game filled with an extensive map filled to the brim with unique enemies and an extensive build-a-weapon system

Shadovis

A more classic take on a roblox rpg, still its a great game with extensive features, map-wise and enemy-wise, however survived longer than its sequel

Shadovia

The sequel of shadovis, as good as its predesessrors but in the end failed because of a declining playerbase of both games

Rune Slayer

The absolute peak of what an rpg should aspire to be community-wise, however the game met its untimely demise because of non frequent updates and multiple bugs, making the game lose its playerbase

World//Zero

A more simplistic take on the genre, however what makes it stand out is its characters, which resemble more anime characters than roblox ones, a decent game at most however still enjoyable

Dungeon Quest

An rpg with limited world-building and next to no story, however it makes up for its losses in its theme, which in this case is dungeons, Explore them and beat enemies and lastly bosses for items, and ofcourse have fun

DeepWoken

A perma death game filled with world exploration ad a payed entrance to the game, this resulting in it having lesser players than most but an extremely loyal playerbase

Arcane odyssey

Arcane odyssey is an rpg filled to the brim with story and exploration, however its pvp has a high skill ceiling and could be seen as lackluster, this game however makes up for this in its extensive lore connecting to other rpg games with the same devolopers
